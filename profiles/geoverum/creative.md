# Geoverum Creative Direction Specification

The engine loads all `- ` bullet lines in this document as `[draft] rules` via `rules_file = "geoverum/creative.md"`. A `{{table.key}}` placeholder is replaced by that value of the profile that loads the file, so the 9:16 and 16:9 profiles each quote their own caption and title sizes; write a number that differs per format as a placeholder, never as a literal. Keep channel identity, default staging behaviors, and storyboard alternatives explicit so any third-party engineer, director, or AI agent can interpret them without ambiguity. Defaults serve as baseline staging principles rather than rigid blocking validators; any intentional creative departure must be documented in the segment's `intent` (and `intent.reason`) field. Factual accuracy, licensed asset provenance, and render integrity are mandatory and never waived by creative intent.

## Identity

- Identity: explain geography, borders, climate, hydrology, and history with high-contrast cartographic precision strictly grounded in the supplied narration, script, and verified evidence; never invent place names, dates, quantities, or spatial scales, and never alter locked voiceover timing.
- Identity: open within the first 1.5 seconds on an already-moving dark satellite basemap featuring an immediate visual paradox or spatial contradiction (such as a glowing latitude/border line, crossed-out bridge markers along a major river, a comparative title equation whose equals sign is struck through into `≠`, or an active hazard zone) and terminate abruptly on the final factual punchline without any call-to-action or subscribe screen.

## Defaults

- Default, not a mandate: favor a continuous multi-stop camera trajectory (`map_path` using `flow`, `glide`, `reveal`, `settle`, or `whip` moves combined with 2.5D `tilt` and `roll` banking) across a single dark satellite basemap so the camera moves on most frames (the reference Shorts move on a median 77% of frames; gate P01 warns below `moving_warn = 0.65`) and any camera hold stays under `hold_warn_s = 3.0` s while an overlay keeps animating (a new visual event at least every `max_gap_s = 3.0` s), cutting away to full-screen non-map scenes only for dedicated evidence beats (such as 2D/3D geological cross-sections, archival treaties and parchment maps, or real-world documentary footage).
- Default, not a mandate: anchor every spoken number, distance, temperature, percentage, or historical epoch on screen within 1.5–2.5 seconds of its spoken cue using a dedicated visual actor—specifically counting `map_figures`, double-arrow geodesic measurement bars with dual `km`/`mi` units, animated `map_thermometers`, dark banner pills in the entity's colour (charcoal `#18181c` without an entity), 45-degree diagonal `hatch` fills for contested or submerged zones, and white-bordered cutout props, maritime vessels, moving flag badges, or concise comic `map_bubbles`.
- Default, not a mandate: stage every beat in reading order on its spoken words: the region fill first, its pin about 0.15 s later and the pin label 0.3 s after that, then the title or number on the frame where its word is spoken (`title_at_frame`, figure `at_frame`); titles are Archivo Black {{layout.sizes.title}} px white that track in above their region (`title_entrance = "track"`, `title_anchor = "region"`), region and status names are pills that grow with their typed text (`label_box_entrance = "type"`), numbers count up in signal yellow (`motion = "counter"`, `figure_accent = "#FFD417"`) or pop without an elastic overshoot (`figure_pop = "back"`), and no more than two elements enter at once (`[layout.budget] animating = 2`).

## Alternatives

- Alternatives: generate three structurally distinct storyboard concepts over the same locked voiceover: `grounded` (continuous satellite fly-through punctuated by archival document, framed photo inset, and documentary footage evidence beats); `contrast` (split-screen or tilted-latitude comparison, binary policy-zone color division, and side-by-side thermometer or stat-pill juxtaposition); and `bold` (an unbroken macro-continent-to-16x-micro-enclave camera journey or a flat-projection-to-3D-globe great-circle reveal).
- Intent: document the narrative purpose of every shot and overlay. Render burned-in captions as single-line phrases (`max_words = {{caption_style.max_words}}`, Inter Bold {{caption_style.size}} px white with a soft drop shadow and no backing box by default) anchored in the lower placement zone (`fixed_y = {{caption_style.fixed_y}}`), permit planned visual overlap (`reference_style.overlap.allowed = true`) whenever foreground-to-background text contrast remains legible, and keep `creative_autofix` disabled unless explicitly requested.
