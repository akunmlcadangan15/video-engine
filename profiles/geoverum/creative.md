# Geoverum creative direction (single source)

The engine loads the `- ` lines as `[draft] rules` via `rules_file`. Keep identity, defaults and alternatives explicit. Defaults are starting points, not universal validators; an intentional departure belongs in the segment intent and rationale. Facts, licensed sources and render integrity are never waived by creative intent.

## Identity

- Identity: explain geography accurately from the supplied narration, script and evidence; do not invent names, dates, quantities or scale. Keep information readable and spatial relationships honest; do not alter locked voiceover to fit a template.
- Identity: keep the Geoverum documentary voice and brand recognisable. Prefer a purposeful ending without a call to action; a keyword callback is optional, not a required final frame.

## Defaults

- Default, not a mandate: map-led explanation with one clear focal point, typography suited to the surface, consistent semantic colours and a small readable set of labels. Use footage as evidence when it communicates better; a map, still, comparison or footage-led opener is equally valid.
- Default, not a mandate: move or introduce information when the thought changes, and support claims with sourced visual evidence. Choose cuts, holds and reveals for comprehension, not a quota of new elements every few seconds; deliberate static shots, negative space and longer reading holds are valid.

## Alternatives

- Alternatives: propose three genuinely different films over the same locked words: grounded documentary/evidence-led holds; contrast-led comparison; bold minimalist reveal or a purposeful camera journey. They must differ in storytelling strategy and pacing, not just colours, effects or transitions; do not force Hook/Locate/Count/Cases/Cause/Impact/Twist on every topic.
- Intent: explain why each shot exists. For a deliberate static shot or empty area declare segment intent with a reason; use motion moving only where visible change is expected. Camera intensity must not be raised merely to satisfy a metric; creative_autofix stays false unless explicitly requested. Use the supported scene vocabulary, with effects only when they clarify the idea.
