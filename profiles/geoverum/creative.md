# Geoverum creative rules (single source)

Hard creative rules for `engine draft` on the Geoverum channel. This file is the
single source of truth for these rules: `profiles/geoverum.toml` points here via
`[draft] rules_file`, and the engine loads the `- ` lines below as
`profile.draft.rules`. Do not copy these rules into `profiles/taste/*.json`
(`rules` there are learned preferences only) or into `video_engine/director/guidance.md`
(global principles only). One rule per `- ` line; keep each rule to one line.

- Open on a moving map (kind map, camera zoom_in or a pan) with a geographical paradox, visual pun, stark dividing line, or magnifying-glass mystery and the first pin, route, or card within 1.5 s; narration and the first caption start at 0.0-0.2 s.
- Use HyperFrames scenes as the primary visual composer (satellite maps, historical parchment maps, 2D side-view cross-section diagrams, 3D space globes, cards, and props). Move between places on one map with a map_path (zooming up to 16x for enclaves/straits) or cut freely whenever the narrative rhythm calls for a new shot.
- Something new on screen every 1-3 s: a pin with dot-and-leader label, a glowing or striped region fill, a white-bordered cutout sticker, a flag-character, a route or dimension bar (mi / km), an animated counter or stat pill, a true-scale polygon drag/tile comparison, or a new shot.
- Match typography to the surface: geometric sans-serif (Inter Bold / SemiBold) on dark satellite, globe, radar, and cross-section scenes (ALL CAPS for macro countries/mountains, Title Case for features/cities/peaks, yellow initial letters for acronyms), and serif/engraved type (EB Garamond / Playfair Display / Cinzel) strictly on historical parchment or blueprint maps.
- The last 5 s show a keyword or the title of the hook again on screen (pin label, card, icon equation, 3D globe summary, or overlay text).
- No call to action and no end card.
